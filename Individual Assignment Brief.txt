Assignment Brief – Individual AI Security Audit and Vulnerability Assessment 
Information Retrieval and Web Analytics (IT3041) 
Lecturer in Charge: 
Mr. Samadhi Chathuranga Rathnayake 
Assignment Overview 
Building an intelligent AI system is only one aspect of modern software engineering. Before 
deployment, every AI system must undergo rigorous security, privacy, ethical, and reliability 
evaluations to ensure that it behaves safely under both normal and adversarial conditions. 
This assignment requires each student to independently perform a professional vulnerability 
assessment of the Agentic AI system developed during the group assignment. Students 
will act as AI Security Analysts (Red Team Members) and evaluate the system from a 
designated security perspective. 
The objective is not to redesign or improve the existing system, but to critically assess its 
robustness by identifying vulnerabilities, evaluating associated risks, and recommending 
appropriate mitigation strategies. 
Each student will submit an independent AI Vulnerability Assessment Report and defend 
their findings during an individual viva examination. 
 
Learning Objectives 
Upon successful completion of this assignment, students should be able to: 
• 
Perform systematic security evaluations of AI systems.  
• 
Apply AI Red Teaming techniques to identify system vulnerabilities.  
• 
Assess AI systems against Responsible AI principles.  
• 
Evaluate the robustness of Large Language Model (LLM)-based applications.  
• 
Conduct structured risk assessments.  
• 
Recommend practical mitigation strategies.  
• 
Produce professional vulnerability assessment reports.  
 
Assignment Structure 
The same project groups formed for the Group Assignment will be used. 
Each student within a group will be assigned one primary evaluation specialization by the 
lecturer. While each student will focus on a different specialization, every student is 
expected to understand the complete system architecture and operation. 
Each student must independently perform all required testing related to the assigned 
specialization and prepare an individual report.
